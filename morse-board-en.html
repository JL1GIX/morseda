▸ ABOUT MORSE BOARD ◂

1. What is Morse Board

Morse Board is a physical paddle (keyer) device for keying real Morse code by hand. Your keying is decoded inside the Morse Board itself, and the corresponding characters are sent to your PC as ordinary keyboard input.

2. Connecting

Just connect it to your PC with a USB cable. No special drivers are needed — it is recognized automatically, just like a regular keyboard.

3. Using it with Morse Da

  • Turn on "⌨️ Morse Board Mode" in the settings screen of Transmit Mode or Fun Mode (not available in Receive Mode)
  • With it on, instead of sending dots/dashes with the Space/J keys, you key directly on the Morse Board's paddle
  • Characters decoded by Morse Board are judged directly as your answer
  • In-game actions such as starting, returning to the lobby, and focusing the nickname field can also be done by keying the corresponding character on the paddle. See the How to Use page's shortcut list for details
📱 On mobile/touch devices, Morse Board Mode is off by default to prevent accidental input. Turn it on manually if needed.
