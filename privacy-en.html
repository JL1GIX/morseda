Basic Policy

This site, "Morse Da" (referred to below as "this site"), is a web app for practicing Morse code for amateur radio. The operator (JL1GIX) respects the privacy of users and is committed to the appropriate handling of personal information.

This site may use access analytics tools to understand how the site is used. These tools use cookies to collect data, but they do not collect any personally identifiable information.
